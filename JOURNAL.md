# November 18th: Initial schematic!
![schematic](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a1f092-0444-752e-a148-d0fdd42def7d/2026-11-18_13-29-59.png)
Today (and a little bit yesterday), I somehow managed to learn how to use KiCad to sketch a schematic without much struggling. It's a pretty simple circuit, but I'm still proud of it. I also found all of my parts, and I'm getting them all on Digikey to save on shipping. (which has a $5 shipping option but it's kind of hidden and took me a while to find it). I was originally going to make it battery powered but I decided against doing that because a) it would add more cost and b) I'd rather just have it be plugged in than have one more thing to need to change the battery and forget about.
**Time spent: 2h**
